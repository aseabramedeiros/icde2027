## Knowledge Models

This directory contains the knowledge artifacts constructed by the methodology and used during Knowledge Exploration and Evaluation.

The models are organized according to the three knowledge-construction stages defined in the paper:

- **Semantic Onboarding**, which transforms heterogeneous organizational   information into ontology-grounded organizational knowledge;
- **Ontological Unpacking**, which makes explicit the semantics underlying the Business Intent and produces the Decision Model; and
- **Semantic Bridging**, which establishes how decision requirements can be grounded in organizational knowledge.
